## Problem

Long-form papers rarely convey the practical trade-offs behind research decisions, making it hard for practitioners and students to apply the work.

## Approach

- Scripted short video explainers and accompanying posts that walk through experiments, failures, and what changed as a result.
- Released updates chronologically so viewers can track how ideas evolved across projects and deployments.
- Highlighted actionable takeaways, open-source resources, and next steps for collaborators.

## Impact

- Helped collaborators and recruiters quickly understand the relevance of ongoing research.
- Created a reusable knowledge base for students and interns ramping up on the work.
- Differentiated the portfolio with transparent, human explanations alongside formal papers.
