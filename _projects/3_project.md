## Problem

Clinicians in memory clinics juggle patient wellbeing, carer input, and time pressure. Traditional assistants fail to manage overlapping speech, interruptions, and sensitive topics in clinical settings.

## Approach

- Built an LLM-powered dialogue manager that could track speakers, repair interruptions, and adapt language for cognitive impairments.
- Combined speech diarisation, intent understanding, and safety constraints to keep the robot’s contributions concise and supportive.
- Collaborated with hospital staff to script handovers, escalation paths, and accessibility safeguards before live deployment.

## Impact

- Deployed in a real hospital memory clinic, demonstrating reliable multi-party interaction with vulnerable users.
- Shared findings through video demos, conference presentations, and publications to guide future clinical robotics.
- Informed broader best practices for deploying LLMs in safety-critical, accessibility-first environments.
