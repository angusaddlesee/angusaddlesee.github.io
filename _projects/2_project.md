## Problem
Memory clinics need technology that can assist clinicians without overwhelming patients with cognitive impairments. Existing conversational systems rarely account for multi-party hospital dynamics or accessibility constraints.

## Approach
- Co-designed dialogue policies with clinicians to keep interactions clear, calm, and clinically appropriate.
- Integrated multimodal sensing and speech technologies into a social robot that could participate in clinician–patient–carer conversations.
- Ran iterative hospital trials to capture real constraints, then refined language understanding and turn-taking for dementia care.

## Impact
- Demonstrated accessible multi-party dialogue in live hospital environments, informing how social robots should support dementia assessments.
- Produced data and methods that influenced PhD research on accessibility-first conversational AI.
- Strengthened collaboration between clinicians, roboticists, and NLP researchers on safe deployment practices.
