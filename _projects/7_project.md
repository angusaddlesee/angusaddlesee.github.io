## Problem

Health and government teams needed actionable insights from fragmented data, but existing tools were slow to deploy and hard for non-technical staff to trust.

## Approach

- Built secure data pipelines that cleaned and standardised multi-source information for clinicians and policymakers.
- Prototyped predictive models with explainable outputs to support triage and planning.
- Co-created dashboards and training so staff could audit outputs and provide feedback loops.

## Impact

- Delivered production-ready analytics that informed frontline decision-making.
- Improved transparency and trust by pairing models with human-readable explanations.
- Established a repeatable approach for responsible ML delivery in the public sector.
