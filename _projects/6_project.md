## Problem

Most dialogue datasets ignore overlapping speech and shifting goals, making it hard to train assistants that must handle real-world clinical conversations.

## Approach

- Collected and annotated multi-party hospital dialogues with diarisation, goals, and accessibility cues.
- Compared pre-training, fine-tuning, and prompt engineering strategies for multi-party goal tracking.
- Released evaluation protocols to stress-test diarisation and turn-taking in safety-critical settings.

## Impact

- Provided a benchmark that better reflects the noise, overlaps, and sensitivities of clinical interaction.
- Highlighted strengths and gaps of LLM approaches for multi-speaker understanding.
- Guided subsequent dataset and model choices for the SPRING project and related research.
