## Problem

Conversational AI teams across academia and industry often work in silos, slowing down the exchange of ideas and responsible deployment practices.

## Approach

- Co-founded regional summits to bring researchers, startups, and large organisations together around applied conversational AI.
- Curated programmes with accessibility, safety, and real-world deployment as central themes.
- Facilitated workshops and panels that paired technical deep-dives with practitioner case studies.

## Impact

- Created recurring events that fostered collaborations across continents.
- Surfaced underrepresented perspectives on accessibility and safety in dialogue systems.
- Built a network that informs hiring, mentorship, and project partnerships.
