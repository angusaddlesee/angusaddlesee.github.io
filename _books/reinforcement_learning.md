The foundational text on reinforcement learning by two pioneers who shaped the field. Covers multi-armed bandits, Markov decision processes, dynamic programming, Monte Carlo methods, temporal-difference learning, and function approximation. The 2nd edition adds coverage of deep RL and policy gradient methods.
