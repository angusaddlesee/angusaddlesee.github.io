About

I build conversational AI that benefits everyone. My work focuses on making large language model systems fast, accessible, and human-centred — from hospital memory clinics to global voice assistants.

bcbd11d5f8a41f098b603d84a28da16fac47b541

I’m currently an Applied Scientist at Amazon Alexa, where I specialise in LLM routing and optimisation. I design systems that intelligently select the right model for each task, balancing accuracy, latency, and cost to deliver faster, more reliable conversational experiences. This work directly impacts Alexa’s responsiveness, scalability, and accessibility for millions of users.

Before joining Amazon, I was a Research Associate working in a hospital environment as part of the EU-funded SPRING project. There, I integrated conversational AI and robotics to support clinicians, caregivers, and patients in memory-clinic settings. This built directly on my PhD in Artificial Intelligence, where I developed accessible dialogue systems tailored to people living with dementia and other cognitive impairments.

Earlier in my career, I designed and delivered machine-learning pipelines for the NHS and the Scottish Government, applying data-driven methods to real public-sector challenges. Across academia and industry, I’ve consistently worked at the intersection of research and deployment, with a strong emphasis on usability, inclusion, and technical rigour.
