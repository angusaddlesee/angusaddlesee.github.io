## Student Supervision

I have supervised **over 60 MSc students** on research projects spanning conversational AI, accessibility, and human-computer interaction. Several of these projects resulted in published papers at international venues.

### Notable Supervision Outcomes

- **Kitchen Accessibility Research**: Supervised 30 MSc students developing voice assistants for visually impaired people, resulting in two published papers at ICMI and ReInAct.
- **Multi-party Dialogue**: Guided students on agreement detection and goal tracking in group conversations.
- **Voice Assistant Accessibility**: Mentored projects on making voice assistants more inclusive for users with cognitive and sensory differences.

## Mentorship Philosophy

I believe in hands-on mentorship that connects academic research with real-world impact. My approach focuses on:

- **Research skills**: Helping students develop rigorous experimental methodology
- **Communication**: Supporting students in writing and presenting their work effectively
- **Career development**: Connecting students with industry opportunities and research networks
- **Ethical awareness**: Ensuring students consider the broader implications of their work
