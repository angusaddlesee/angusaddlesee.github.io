publications

Publications and articles on conversational AI, LLM routing, and accessibility, listed in reverse chronological order.

Last updated: March 2025

2025

  1. Socially Pertinent Robots in Gerontological Healthcare
    Xavier Alameda-Pineda, Angus Addlesee, Daniel Hernández García, and 41 more authors
    In International Journal of Social Robotics, 2025
    Abs

    Reports clinical learnings from deploying socially aware robots in gerontological healthcare, summarising behaviours that kept interactions supportive and safe.

  2. Building for Speech: Designing the Next Generation of Social Robots for Audio Interaction
    Angus Addlesee and Ioannis Papaioannou
    In Frontiers in Robotics and AI, 2025
    Abs

    Outlines design patterns for speech-first social robots, focusing on robust audio perception, dialogue turn-taking, and user trust.

2024

  1. Incremental multi-party conversational AI for people with dementia
    Angus Addlesee
    Heriot-Watt University, 2024
    Abs

    Doctoral work on incremental dialogue systems that manage multi-party hospital conversations while adapting to cognitive impairments.

  2. You Have Interrupted Me Again!: Making Voice Assistants More Dementia-Friendly With Incremental Clarification
    Angus Addlesee and Arash Eshghi
    Frontiers in Dementia, 2024
    Abs

    Demonstrates how incremental clarification strategies let voice assistants recover from interruptions and better support users with dementia.

  3. Multi-party Multimodal Conversations Between Patients, Their Companions, and a Social Robot in a Hospital Memory Clinic
    Angus Addlesee, Neeraj Cherakara, Nivan Nelson, and 5 more authors
    In Proceedings of the 18th Conference of the European Chapter of the Association for Computational Linguistics (EACL), 2024
    Abs

    Presents recordings and analysis of multi-party hospital conversations, highlighting requirements for socially aware clinical robots.

  4. Clarifying Completions: Evaluating How LLMs Respond to Incomplete Questions
    Angus Addlesee, Oliver Lemon, and Arash Eshghi
    In Proceedings of the Joint International Conference on Computational Linguistics, Language Resources and Evaluation (LREC-COLING), 2024
    Abs

    Evaluates prompting and repair strategies that help LLMs handle partial or disrupted user queries without hallucinating.

  5. A Multi-party Conversational Social Robot Using LLMs
    Angus Addlesee, Neeraj Cherakara, Nivan Nelson, and 6 more authors
    In Companion of the 2024 ACM/IEEE International Conference on Human-Robot Interaction (HRI), 2024
    Abs

    Shows how an LLM-driven social robot coordinated with clinicians and carers in real hospital settings.

  6. Self-ownership, not self-production, modulates bias and agency over a synthesised voice
    Bryony Payne, Angus Addlesee, Verena Rieser, and 1 more author
    Cognition, 2024
    Abs DOI

    Explores how perceptions of voice ownership shape user bias and sense of agency toward synthesised speech.

  7. A Holistic Evaluation Methodology for Multi-Party Spoken Conversational Agents
    Nancie Gunson, Angus Addlesee, Daniel Hernández Garcia, and 2 more authors
    In Proceedings of the 24th International Conference on Intelligent Virtual Agents (IVA), 2024
    Abs

    Introduces an evaluation framework covering diarisation, goal tracking, and human factors for multi-party conversational agents.

  8. Grounding LLMs to In-prompt Instructions: Reducing Hallucinations Caused by Static Pre-training Knowledge
    Angus Addlesee
    In Proceedings of the 3rd workshop on Safety for Conversational AI (Safety4ConvAI), 2024
    Abs

    Shows how prompt-level grounding curbs hallucinations by reinforcing user constraints without retraining models.

  9. A Multi-party Dialogue Dataset for Dialogue Goal Tracking in a Hospital Setting and How It Can Be Used in LLM Prompt Engineering Experiments
    Weronika Sieińska, Angus Addlesee, Daniel Hernández Garcia, and 4 more authors
    In Proceedings of the 28th Workshop on the Semantics and Pragmatics of Dialogue (SemDial), 2024
    Abs

    Releases a hospital dialogue dataset and demonstrates how it stress-tests LLM prompt engineering for goal tracking.

2023

  1. Multi-party Goal Tracking with LLMs: Comparing Pre-training, Fine-tuning, and Prompt Engineering
    Angus Addlesee, Weronika Sieińska, Nancie Gunson, and 3 more authors
    In Proceedings of the 24th Annual Meeting of the Special Interest Group on Discourse and Dialogue, 2023
    Abs

    Compares three strategies for tracking conversational goals in multi-speaker hospital dialogues using LLMs.

  2. Detecting agreement in multi-party dialogue: evaluating speaker diarisation versus a procedural baseline to enhance user engagement
    Angus Addlesee, Daniel Denley, Andy Edmondson, and 9 more authors
    In Proceedings of the workshop on advancing GROup UNderstanding and robots aDaptive behaviour (GROUND), 2023
    Abs

    Benchmarks diarisation-led approaches for detecting agreement in multi-party dialogue against simpler procedural baselines.

  3. Detecting Agreement in Multi-party Conversational AI
    Laura Schauer, Jason Sweeny, Charlie Lyttle, and 10 more authors
    In Proceedings of the workshop on advancing GROup UNderstanding and robots aDaptive behaviour (GROUND), 2023
    Abs

    Presents student-led experiments on recognising agreement signals to keep group conversations flowing smoothly.

  4. Understanding Disrupted Sentences Using Underspecified Abstract Meaning Representation
    Angus Addlesee and Marco Damonte
    In Proceedings of INTERSPEECH 2023, 2023
    Abs DOI

    Uses underspecified AMR to recover meaning from disrupted speech, improving robustness for conversational agents.

  5. Understanding and Answering Incomplete Questions
    Angus Addlesee and Marco Damonte
    In Proceedings of the 5th Conference on Conversational User Interfaces, 2023
    Abs

    Proposes methods for interpreting incomplete or interrupted questions so assistants can respond without forcing restarts.

  6. Building for Speech: Designing the Next Generation of Social Robots for Audio Interaction
    Angus Addlesee, Ioannis Papaioannou, and Oliver Lemon
    In Proceedings of the 2nd Workshop on Working with Trouble and Failures in Conversation Between Humans and Robots (WTF), 2023
    Abs

    Shares lessons from audio-first robot design, focusing on failure recovery and conversational repair.

  7. Voice Assistant Accessibility
    Angus Addlesee
    In Proceedings of the 13th International Workshop on Spoken Dialogue Systems Technology (IWSDS), 2023
    Abs

    Summarises accessibility requirements for voice assistants serving users with cognitive or sensory differences.

  8. Data Collection for Multi-party Task-based Dialogue in Social Robotics
    Angus Addlesee, Weronika Sieińska, Nancie Gunson, and 3 more authors
    In Proceedings of the 13th International Workshop on Spoken Dialogue Systems Technology (IWSDS), 2023
    Abs

    Details a data collection pipeline for multi-party robot dialogues, capturing turn-taking and goal progress.

2022

  1. Securely Capturing People’s Interactions with Voice Assistants at Home: A Bespoke Tool for Ethical Data Collection
    Angus Addlesee
    In Proceedings of the Second Workshop on NLP for Positive Impact (NLP4PI), 2022
    Abs

    Introduces a privacy-preserving toolkit for collecting in-home voice assistant data with informed consent.

  2. A Visually-Aware Conversational Robot Receptionist
    Nancie Gunson, Daniel Hernández Garcia, Weronika Sieińska, and 5 more authors
    In Proceedings of the 23rd Annual Meeting of the Special Interest Group on Discourse and Dialogue, 2022
    Abs

    Combines visual perception with dialogue to create a receptionist robot that can manage visitors in dynamic spaces.

  3. Understanding Partial Questions
    Angus Addlesee and Marco Damonte
    In Proceedings of the 10th Amazon Machine Learning Conference, 2022
    Abs

    Analyses how assistants can infer intent from partial questions and proposes strategies to elicit the missing context.

2021

  1. Am i allergic to this? assisting sight impaired people in the kitchen
    Elisa Ramil Brick, Vanesa Caballero Alonso, Conor O’Brien, and 5 more authors
    In Proceedings of the 2021 International Conference on Multimodal Interaction, 2021
    Abs

    Builds a multimodal assistant that checks ingredients and safety information to support visually impaired cooks.

  2. The spoon is in the sink: Assisting visually impaired people in the kitchen
    Katie Baker, Amit Parekh, Adrien Fabre, and 3 more authors
    In Proceedings of the Reasoning and Interaction Conference (ReInAct 2021), 2021
    Abs

    Demonstrates how spatial reasoning and dialogue can help blind users locate kitchen objects.

  3. Incremental Graph-Based Semantics and Reasoning for Conversational AI
    Angus Addlesee and Arash Eshghi
    In Proceedings of the Reasoning and Interaction Conference (ReInAct 2021), 2021
    Abs

    Uses incremental graph-based semantics to support real-time reasoning in conversational agents.

2020

  1. A comprehensive evaluation of incremental speech recognition and diarization for conversational AI
    Angus Addlesee, Yanchao Yu, and Arash Eshghi
    In Proceedings of the 28th International Conference on Computational Linguistics, 2020
    Abs

    Benchmarks incremental ASR and diarisation pipelines to understand their effect on downstream dialogue quality.

  2. Ethically Collecting Multi-Modal Spontaneous Conversations with People that have Cognitive Impairments
    Angus Addlesee and Pierre Albert
    In LREC 2020 Workshop Language Resources and Evaluation Conference 11–16 May 2020, 2020
    Abs

    Describes consent, anonymisation, and data handling practices for recording conversations with vulnerable participants.

2019

  1. Current Challenges in Spoken Dialogue Systems and Why They Are Critical for Those Living with Dementia
    Angus Addlesee, Arash Eshghi, and Ioannis Konstas
    In Dialogue for Good (DiGo), 2019
    Abs

    Highlights dialogue system gaps that disproportionately affect people with dementia and proposes research priorities.
